## 🔴 Issue 1 — Phase detector locks onto the WRONG swing on multi-event clips

**This was originally flagged as an "fps bug." It is not.** Evidence from the
fingerprints disproves a frame-rate-math error:

- At the SAME ~58fps, some clips are near-perfect (`julio_rodriguez` foot_plant 1400 vs ground-truth 1409, off 9) while others are catastrophic (`steven_kwan` detected at 1715 vs labeled 1308; `kyle_tucker` 827 vs 473). A systematic fps error would shift *all* of them proportionally — instead it's **bimodal**.
- The catastrophic clips show internally *coherent* phases (load→plant→launch→contact in order) — just at the wrong location. The detector found *a* swing, not *the* labeled one.
- `my_swing` is **30fps** and still failed (locked onto motion at frames 0–34, real swing at 183).

**Root cause:** on clips containing multiple motion events (broadcast replays/cuts,
or pre-swing setup movement) the burst selector picks the wrong event.

**Why I didn't fix it:** there's no safe change that addresses this without risking
the path that already works. Your product's real input — clean single-swing ~30fps
phone clips — is mostly accurate (median 2-frame foot-plant error, 64% within ±3).
A burst-selection rewrite needs your call + careful before/after validation.

**Recommended next step:** improve swing-event selection (e.g., anchor to a hint, or
score candidate bursts by swing-signature coherence), measured before/after with the
harness. Good news: the harness can now measure this precisely.

## 🔴 Issue 2 — The MLB reference library is corrupted (and the source clips are gone)

All 17 MLB references in `references/*.json` were built (via
`build_reference_library.py` → `detect_phases.py`) from **50–60fps** clips with the
same wrong-burst detector. They carry wrong phase frames — several store implausible
foot_plant frames (torkelson 1342, lindor 1012 = 17+ seconds into a long slow-mo
clip = wrong burst). Proven: `aaron_judge.json` stores foot_plant 44, but hand-labeled
ground truth for that exact clip is 132; `mookie_betts.json` stores 861, reprocessing
gives 0.

**Blast radius:** `mlb_match_stats.json` (the entire z-score space every user is
compared against), the user-vs-pro metric comparisons, and pro-anchored thresholds in
`swing_score.py` are all built on this bad data. Match percentages shown to users are
not trustworthy.

**Also a real, isolated bug:** `mlb_match.py:18` `movement_vector()` reads `timing_ms`
(uncorrected) instead of `timing_ms_corrected` for the 9 slow-mo refs. **Do not fix it
in isolation** — `mlb_match_stats.json` was computed the same (buggy) way, so a one-line
fix without recomputing the stats creates an inconsistency.

**Why I didn't fix it:** 14 of 17 source MLB videos are **absent from disk** (only
judge/judge_copy/mookie survive). Rebuilding correctly requires you to re-source the
clips, *then* fix the detector (Issue 1), *then* rebuild references + recompute
`mlb_match_stats.json` (its compute script isn't even in the repo) + the mlb_match.py
fix + re-examine swing_score thresholds. That's a coordinated rebuild gated on data
only you can provide.
